## Shell structure

- `ShellRoute` 1 (`app_router.dart:226`): customer app shell `AQAppShell` (`features/workspace/aq_app_shell.dart`), floating four-tab glass bar; also hosts `extraShellRoutes()` and `dbShellRoutes()` (customer-side pages that keep the bar).
- `ShellRoute` 2 (`app_router.dart:372`): business shell `ProShell` (`features/pro/pro_shell.dart`), role bar or rail; hosts `dbProShellRoutes()`.
- `ShellRoute` 3 (`app_router.dart:422`): agent shell `AgentShell` (`features/agent/agent_shell.dart`), hosts `agentShellRoutes()`.
- Outside any shell: entry, auth, onboarding, signup, verification, legal, `/admin` (host of the Admin app), `/shop`, `/work`.
- The Admin Console is a separate `GoRouter` (`admin/admin_app.dart: buildAdminRouter`) with its own `AdminFrame`; it is reachable at `#/admin` in the customer build and as its own bundle (`lib/admin_main.dart`).
